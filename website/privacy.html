PRIVACY

Roadie collects nothing.

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Roadie talks only to the services below, and only to do what you asked.

What Roadie connects to

  • Your Music Assistant servers, if you add one. To browse your library, control players and stream audio.
  • The Winamp Skin Museum. Only when you open it, to show and download skins. Requests identify themselves as Roadie/<version>.
  • roadie.sstools.co. To check for updates. The check sends the app’s version and no system profile.

On your Mac

  • Location is used only to read your Wi-Fi network’s name, so Roadie can pick the right server. It is never stored or sent.
  • Music Assistant tokens are kept in your login keychain.
  • Settings are stored in Roadie’s preferences (co.sstools.roadie).
  • Your play queue and skin index are kept in ~/Library/Application Support/co.sstools.roadie.
  • Caches (skin thumbnails, the folder index and Museum previews) are kept in ~/Library/Caches/co.sstools.roadie and can be deleted at any time.
  • Skins you download from the Museum are saved in a Museum folder inside the skins folder you chose.
