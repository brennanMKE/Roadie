## Description

#0212's baseline has `WindowDockingCoordinator` at 56 % and `SkinWindowController` at 71 %. `MainShadeView` (128 lines), `LegacyDataCleanup` (79) and `FileInfoPanel` (52) are at 0 %. #0211 left two follow-ups here: `WindowLayoutTests` (`SkinWindowController.applyLayout` at scale 1 and 2 and in shade, and the `windowLayout` round trip), and `LegacyDataCleanupTests`, which needs an injectable Keychain and caches root.

## Plan

1. `Tests/RoadieTests/WindowLayoutTests.swift`: build the three `SkinWindowController`s off screen with a base skin. Check `applyLayout` frames at scale 1 and 2 and in shade. Check the `windowLayout` persistence round trip (save, new model, restore: the same frames and shade states).
2. `WindowDockingCoordinator`: `dragBegan`/`drag(to:)`/`dragEnded` move the docked windows together and snap at the threshold. `showPosition(for:below:)` and `place(_:below:)` put a window under its reference. Use the controllers from step 1; the geometry rules themselves are already tested in `WindowDocking`.
3. `LegacyDataCleanup.run`: inject a Keychain remover (`() -> String` status, defaulting to the real `SecItemDelete` query) and a caches root URL. Test file: `Tests/RoadieTests/LegacyDataCleanupTests.swift`. Check that:
   - the Plex defaults keys and a stored `plex` source are removed and a `folder` choice is kept
   - the `Tracks` cache directory under the injected root is deleted
   - `versionKey` makes the second run a no-op
   - the real Keychain is never touched in tests
4. `MainShadeView`/`MiniTimeView`: the time text and position-slider value for elapsed and remaining, and the transport buttons' commands. Move them into `static` helpers if they are inline.
5. `FileInfoPanel.show(_:)`: the panel's fields for a `TrackInfo` fixture (title, format, bitrate, path), built headless.

## Tests

`applyLayoutAtScaleOneAndTwo`, `applyLayoutShaded`, `windowLayoutRoundTrips`, `dockedWindowsMoveTogether`, `placeBelowReference`, `legacyCleanupRemovesPlexState`, `legacyCleanupKeepsFolderChoice`, `legacyCleanupRunsOnce`, `miniTimeText`, `fileInfoFields`. Expect list: all of them.

## Which tier

**Fast:** `scripts/verify.sh --expect <the tests above>`. The new tests must not need the network, a real Keychain or a speaker. They use the stubs and injectable seams the plan names.
