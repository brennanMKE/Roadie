## Root cause

The Library had no Favorites section. The backend API could favorite an item (#0141) but had no way to list favorites or count them.

## Fix

- `MusicBackendKit`: `MusicBackend` gains `favoriteCount(_:)` and `favoriteTracks/Albums/Artists/Playlists(page:)`. The defaults, which refuse as unsupported, are in the new `FavoriteListing.swift`.
- `MusicAssistantKit`: `MARequest.favoriteLibraryItems` sends `library_items` with `favorite: true`, and `MARequest.favoriteCount` sends `count` with `favorite_only: true`. Both are in `MusicAssistantBackend+Favorites.swift`, along with the backend methods. Each page fetches the count alongside it as the total.
- `LibraryBrowser`: `FavoriteKind` covers tracks, albums, artists and playlists. Radio waits for W12. `FavoritesModel` adds per-kind counts, `selectedKind` and four paged lists. A confirmed change flips the heart at once, then refetches that kind's list and the counts, so an unfavorited row leaves the list. `LibrarySection.favorites` appears only with `.favorites`. Selecting a playlist stays in Favorites.
- `StubMusicBackend` lists the library items whose `isFavorite` is true.
- App: a new `FavoritesView.swift` holds the kinds column with counts and the detail list, reusing `PagedListView`, `AlbumListView` and `TrackRow` with their hearts. Albums and playlists open their tracks in place, and an artist opens in Artists. The sidebar and window each got their new case.
