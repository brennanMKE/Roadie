# 0126 — Skins from the Winamp Skin Museum can't be browsed or downloaded in the app

|---|---|

## Description

Requested by the user: "what about getting skins from the Skins Museum? https://skins.webamp.org". Today the Skin Library (⌘K) only shows skins already on disk; getting a new skin means finding it on the Museum in a browser, downloading the `.wsz` and dropping it in. The Museum has about 103,000 classic skins with a public GraphQL API (`docs/winamp-skin-museum-access.md`).

## Expected behavior

- A Museum section in the Skin Library: browse and search the Museum's classic skins with their screenshots, without leaving the app.
- One click downloads a skin into the user's skins folder, indexes it (#0033) and applies it; already-downloaded skins are marked and not downloaded twice (md5).
- NSFW-flagged skins hidden by default, with a setting to show them.
- Works offline-gracefully: an error state, no hang, when the Museum is unreachable. Screenshots and search results are cached.
- Modern (`.wal`) skins are excluded; SkinAmp renders classic skins only.

## Notes

- Tests must not touch the network (the VM guest has none): the API client takes an injectable transport, with recorded fixtures.
- Respect the Museum: paged queries, no bulk crawling from the app, a descriptive User-Agent.
