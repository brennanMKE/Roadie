## Description

Draw the spectrum analyzer and oscilloscope in the main window using `viscolor.txt` colors and AudioCore's analyzer data.

## Acceptance criteria

- Click cycles spectrum → oscilloscope → off, like Winamp.
- Rendering stays under 1 ms per frame.

## Notes

See [docs/plan.md](../docs/plan.md) (M4) and [docs/plex-music-library.md](../docs/plex-music-library.md).

From #0005: the real-time mixer tap delivered 4800-frame buffers at 48 kHz, about 10 Hz of fresh audio. Drawing at 30–60 Hz will likely need the lagging read cursor described in risk 2 of #0005's plan, so frames don't repeat in bursts. See [#0005](0005.md) Gotchas.
