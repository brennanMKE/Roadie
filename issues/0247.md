Each spoils a whole VM run and costs a rerun. Likely causes, from #0149: the shared `MPNowPlayingInfoCenter` in the bridge test; Quick Look checks running before `lsregister` finishes (the most frequent rerun cause as of M15); the progressive render loop stopping a block early under load; and `verify.sh` / `run-tests-vm.sh` not mapping a lost guest agent or a golden image busy being cloned to INFRA.

## Expected behavior

Each passes reliably with two guests running at once:

- The Quick Look step registers the app with `lsregister -f` and polls for the UTType before checking (do this one first).
- `installedBridgePublishesAndClearsInfoCenter` and the progressive render test wait on real conditions, not wall-clock time or a render loop that may stop early.
- A lost guest-agent connection mid-run is reported as INFRA (exit 3, retried; #0122), and the golden-image preflight retries briefly before reporting the image missing.

Every app bug found along the way lands with a regression test proven to fail first.
