## Description

The user wrote: "I do not see how I could possibly manage playlists. I need clarification on that." Roadie follows Winamp's model, but nothing says so:

- **The skinned playlist editor is the play queue.** It's what plays next, not a saved playlist. It shows This Mac's own queue, or the Music Assistant queue of whatever is playing (a speaker, or This Mac fed by Music Assistant).
- **Named playlists live in the Library** (⌘L › Playlists). Today they come from Music Assistant only; the folder library has none.
- **Moving between the two:**
  - Load a playlist into the editor with LIST › Load, Playback › Load Playlist from Library…, a double-click on the playlist, right-click › Play, or by dragging it onto the editor (#0189, #0190).
  - Save the editor's contents with LIST › Save or Playback › Save Queue as Playlist…. That makes a Music Assistant playlist from a Music Assistant queue, or an M3U/PLS file from the local queue.
  - Add Library tracks to a named playlist with right-click › Add to Playlist ▸, or by dragging them onto the playlist in the Library.

This issue writes that down: a help page on the website, and a Help menu item that opens it.

### 1. `website/help.html` (new)

Copy `website/privacy.html`'s structure: the same `<head>` (stylesheet, favicon, manifest), header and footer. One `<main>` with these sections, each an `<h2>` with an `id`:

1. **`#playlists`, "Playlists and the playlist editor."** Short paragraphs, no marketing:
   - "The playlist editor is what's playing." It's the play queue, as in Winamp. Double-click a row to play it; press Q to play a row next; press J (or ⌘J) to find a track in it.
   - "Your saved playlists are in the Library." Open it with ⌘L and choose Playlists. Playlists come from Music Assistant; with only a music folder there's no Playlists section. Save a queue to a file instead (below).
   - "Put a playlist in the editor." Double-click it in the Library, right-click › Play (or Play Next / Add to Queue), drag it onto the playlist editor, or use the editor's LIST › Load › Library Playlist… (Playback › Load Playlist from Library… in the menu bar).
   - "Save what's in the editor." LIST › Save, or Playback › Save Queue as Playlist…. On a Music Assistant queue it becomes a Music Assistant playlist. On your Mac's own queue it's saved as an M3U or PLS file, and LIST › Load › Playlist File… opens it again.
   - "Add songs to a saved playlist." In the Library, right-click tracks or an album › Add to Playlist ▸, or drag them onto the playlist in the Playlists list.
   - "Dragging into the playlist editor." Artists, albums, tracks and playlists from the Library, and files and folders from Finder. On your Mac's own queue they go in where you drop them; on a Music Assistant queue they're added at the end.
2. **`#jump-to-file`, "Jump to File."** It searches only what's in the playlist editor. If the editor is empty, add music from the Library or File › Open first.
