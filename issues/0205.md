## Description

Dragging from the Library (⌘L) to the skinned playlist editor never worked for the user (#0188), and #0184/#0188 were resolved without anyone seeing a drop land. This issue adds `LibraryUITests` over the folder library: opening on the search field, browsing, search, double-click to play, context menus, and the drags from album, track and artist rows onto a playlist row and onto the bottom bar. It is also the answer to #0188's open question (does the AppKit target see the SwiftUI pasteboard?). The Music Assistant-only parts (playlists, favorites, discover, radio, output picker) are #0206. Rows: [docs/design/test-automation.md](../docs/design/test-automation.md) §5.7.

## Root cause

The tests found a real #0188 bug: album rows couldn't be opened with a click on their content, and they couldn't be dragged. `libraryContainerActions` attached the album's double-click as `.simultaneousGesture(TapGesture(count: 2))`. `FavoriteRow` gives the whole row `.contentShape(Rectangle())`, so the gesture's hit area was all of the row's content (art, title and spacer). A click on an album's title or art didn't reach the List's selection, so the album never opened, and the gesture blocked the row's `.draggable`. Artist rows use the same `FavoriteRow` but have no gesture, so they selected and dragged fine. In the narrow Albums column, a click at the row's far right (dx 0.95) landed in the row's padding and still selected. That is why the first attempt's tests passed there but not in the artist detail.

Not bugs:
- **Play Next** appending Tone D at the end is #0181's design: `.next` appends and puts the entries at the front of up-next (`LocalPlayerOutput.play` → `prependUpNext`), and the editor marks them `[1]`.
- **The line-50 timeouts** were the test's `value CONTAINS` predicate over `descendants(matching: .any)`, which stalled the app's accessibility. Label queries over `.any` were fast.
- **Retry and Open Settings… not found:** the identifiers `library.retry` and `library.openSettings` don't exist in the tree. `LibraryStatusView`'s outer `.accessibilityIdentifier("library.status")` overrides them (#0252). The buttons work for users.

Review bounce (Opus medium): with 92f28ee, a click selects an album, and in a NavigationStack album list (an artist's albums, `ArtistAlbumsView`; favorite albums, `FavoritesDetailView`) selecting pushes `TrackListView` at once. The second click of a double-click then lands on the pushed track list, not on the album list's primary action, so the album may not play. `testDoubleClickAlbumInArtistDetailPlays` is the test, and it failed before the fix (see `## Verification`).

## Fix

**App, 92f28ee.** This is #0188's planned branch for "albums don't drag": album lists take their double-click and context menu from the List-level `contextMenu(forSelectionType:primaryAction:)`, as track lists already do.
- `PagedListView` gains optional `primaryAction` and `menu` closures and applies them to its `List`. Lists that pass neither are unchanged.
- `AlbumListView` passes play-the-album as the primary action, and the new `LibraryContainerMenuItems` view as the menu.
- `libraryContainerMenu` now builds its menu from `LibraryContainerMenuItems` too. `libraryContainerActions` is removed.
- `AlbumListView` serves the Albums section, an artist's albums and favorite albums.
- Music Assistant playlist rows still carry the same gesture (#0251).
- Return on a selected album now plays it (the List's primary action), as it already did for tracks.

**App, 670ce7c (review bounce).** `ArtistAlbumsView` and `FavoritesDetailView` push the selected album (or favorite playlist) a double-click interval after the click (`pushAfterDoubleClick` in `AlbumListView.swift`, `NSEvent.doubleClickInterval`), so a double-click's second click still reaches the album list and plays the album. Going back, and a view appearing with an album already selected, are immediate.

**Tests, 6897402,** in `Tests/RoadieUITests/LibraryUITests.swift`:
- `testBrowseArtistAlbumTracks` clicks row titles (`clickTitle`), where a user clicks: it's the regression test for the click.
- `testDragAlbumOntoPlaylistRow` is the regression test for the drag.
- `shows()` replaces `anyText`: a label query on any element plus a value query on static texts only.
- `control()` finds the status view's buttons by label.
- `testContextMenus` asserts Play Next as #0181 defines it (row 3, marker `[1]`) and that Add to Queue leaves no marker.

**Tests, 5e04be1 (review bounce):**
- `testDoubleClickAlbumInArtistDetailPlays` (new): double-clicks Two Discs' title in Tone Artist's detail; the queue becomes D, E, F with current row 0, and the album opens.
- `testSearch` checks Tone A by exact label (`showsExactly`): `shows("Tone A")` also matched Tone Album and Tone Artist. Search rows have no identifiers of their own (`library.results` overrides them).
- `testDoubleClickAlbumPlays`' track part double-clicks Two Discs' Tone E with Tone Album queued, so the queue becoming D, E, F with current row 1 shows the track played within its own album.
- `testContextMenus` also selects Tone Album, right-clicks Two Discs and chooses Add to Queue: D, E, F are appended. The menu acts on the clicked row, not the selection.
- `testDragWithoutDropLeavesTheQueue` only checks that nothing changes; it would also pass if the drag never started (the drag image isn't in the accessibility tree). Said in its doc comment and in design §5.7.

Divergences from the plan, from the code and the fixture layout:

- Methods carry XCTest's `test` prefix (`testDragAlbumOntoPlaylistRow` …); design §5.7 rows renamed to match.
- The folder backend's capabilities are search, artwork and local output, so the sidebar is Artists and Albums only (no Favorites); `Favorite` and `Add to Playlist` are asserted absent from every menu.
- Two Discs has 3 tracks (D, E on disc 1, F on disc 2), not 6: the album drop makes the queue 6 rows with rows 1–3 = D, E, F.
- Search uses "tone a", not "tone c": `FolderBackend.search` is a substring match, Single.m4a is tagged "Tone C" (two tracks), and no album title contains "tone c". "tone a" hits Tone Artist, Tone Album and Tone A only.
- `/nonexistent` is `.unreachable` → Retry only (`LibraryStatusView` shows Retry or Open Settings…, never both); Open Settings… is checked on a relaunch with no folder (`.notConfigured`).
- `testDoubleClickAlbumPlays` starts with Two Discs queued so Tone Album replacing it is visible; it reuses `RoadieUIApp.playToneAlbum`.
- The album-art view has no identifier, so it isn't asserted; `addRadioURL` moved to #0206 (no Radio section without Music Assistant).

## Gotchas

- In SwiftUI on macOS, a `Text`'s string is the static text's `value`, not its `label`. A `value` predicate over `descendants(matching: .any)` timed out and stalled the app's accessibility for later queries.
- A row's `.accessibilityIdentifier` lands on every element in the row. A container's identifier overrides its children's own identifiers (#0252).
