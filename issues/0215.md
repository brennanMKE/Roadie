## Plan

1. `PlayerModel.addToPlaylist(_:_:)` and `createPlaylist(named:adding:)` (`AddToPlaylistMenu.swift`): against `MusicBackendStub`, check that the items reach the right playlist, that a new playlist is created then filled, and that a backend without playlist editing leaves a notice. Test file: `Tests/RoadieTests/LibraryPlaylistActionsTests.swift`.
2. `PlayerModel+Favorites` (same file): `toggleFavorite` favorites all unless every item already is, `setFavorite` posts "Couldn't favorite" when the backend refuses, `canFavoriteCurrentTrack` is false on the local queue, and `favoriteCurrentTrack` posts "Added to favorites" or the backend's error.
3. Move the pure parts out of the views into `static` or `internal` helpers next to them, with no behaviour change, then test them in `Tests/RoadieTests/LibraryViewLogicTests.swift`:
   - `DiscoverRowView.playable(_:in:)`, `request(_:mode:)` and `DiscoverItemRow.symbol` (every case)
   - `FavoriteKindsView`'s titles and symbols
   - `TrackListView`'s `heading`, `albumArtist` and `openPlaylist` choice
   - `PlaylistTracksView.confirmTitle`, and `PlaylistEditingModifier`'s delete title
   - `RadioListView.play` and `AddRadioSheet.add` through `RadioURLInput`
   - `OutputPickerView.name(of:)` and its row and group-row state
   - what `LibraryStatusView` (connecting / failed with Settings / failed with Retry / connected), `PlaylistEditBar` (pending / error / nothing), `PlaylistMessageBar` and `PagedListView`'s footer (loading / error with Retry / nothing) show
   - `SearchResultsView`'s sections for a result set with and without each kind
4. `LibraryPlaybackActions.swift`: the play and enqueue paths its modifiers call (the `PlayRequest`s they build for a track, an album and a playlist).
5. `SkinThumbnailView.swift`: confirm it has no callers, then delete it (`SkinThumbnailPlaceholder` too, unless something uses it by then).
