## Root cause

The playlist detail only listed and played tracks: there was no backend call for `music/playlists/remove_playlist_tracks` or `move_playlist_track`, no editor model to hold optimistic edits, and no remove or reorder UI.

## Fix

- `MusicBackend` gains `remove(positions:fromPlaylist:)` and `movePlaylistTrack(_:from:to:)` (default `.unsupported(.playlistEditing)`). `MusicAssistantBackend` sends 1-based positions, deduplicated and highest first, with the playlist's library id, after checking the playlist is track-editable. Move throws `.unsupported` without sending when #0137's probe lacks `move_playlist_track` (2.10.6). `LibraryPlaylist.editing` now carries `.reorder` from the probe in the list, favorites, get, create and import mappings; `playlists(page:)` waits for the probe.
- New `PlaylistEditor` (`@MainActor @Observable`, owned by `LibraryBrowserModel`) applies remove and move to `rows` at once, then sends through one serialized task chain. `pending` blocks a second structural edit. A failure restores the snapshot and sets `error`, which the bar shows with Retry. A generation counter drops stale refetches. After a remove, the refetch comes from W5's `playlistUpdated` or a 2 s fallback; after a move it runs at once. An `.unsupported` answer turns reorder off for the session.
- `PlaylistPositions`: selection to 1-based positions (descending), renumber after remove, move, and SwiftUI `onMove` to final index.
- UI: `PlaylistTracksView` (split from `TrackListView`) adds the Delete key, which asks first for 10 or more tracks, and the "Remove from Playlist" context item. `onMove` drag is only active when `canReorder`. Move Up/Down items appear only when the playlist has `.reorder`. A one-shot "Reordering needs a newer Music Assistant." caption shows on editable playlists without reorder. `PlaylistEditBar` shows "Removing N tracks…", or the error with Retry/Dismiss.

Review:
- The #0148 cherry-pick merges in `MARequest+Playlists.swift`, `MusicAssistantBackend+Playlists.swift`, `MusicBackend+Playlists.swift`, `MusicBackend.swift`, `StubMusicBackend.swift` and `PlaylistWriteTests.swift` keep both sides. Two leftovers were fixed in f525291: `importPlaylist` mapped without the reorder flag, and `importSendsTheTextWithLibraryMatching` had lost its `disconnect()`.
- Known risks, accepted because reorder is hidden on the user's 2.10.6 server:
  - The playlist rows keep #0136's `.draggable(MediaIDTransfer)` and the list keeps its `dropDestination`. On a server with `move_playlist_track`, a row drag may start a MediaID drag rather than an `onMove` reorder, or land in the list's own drop (MA skips duplicate adds). Check this on a newer server, and switch the rows to `.itemProvider` if it does.
  - On a cold start, the first playlists page may load before the probe has started, so reorder can be missing until the list reloads.
  - The caption shows until dismissed rather than "on first drag", since there is no drag to observe when `onMove` is off.
