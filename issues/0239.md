## Description

Three MilkDrop habits are still missing from the visualization window after #0238:

- **The song title animation.** When the track changed, MilkDrop flew the new title in over the picture; T showed it on demand.
- **Corner readouts.** F2 song title, F3 song time, F4 preset name, F5 frames per second: small texts that stay on screen (unlike Roadie's overlay, which hides).
- **Help.** F1 showed the key list.

Design: [docs/design/visualization.md](../docs/design/visualization.md) §1.5, §2.7.

## Plan

### Song title

- `VisualizationModel` observes the current track identity (`nowPlaying` title + artist, and `player.queue.current?.id` locally) while the window is open; a change (not the first value after opening) with `settings.songTitleOnTrackChange` on, or the `showSongTitle` command (T), starts a title show: text "Artist – Title" (title alone with no artist).
- `VisualizationSongTitleView` (in the overlay hosting view, centred, large, white with a soft shadow, `vis.songTitle`, label = the text): 0.5 s fade in growing from 90 % to 100 %, 2 s hold, 1 s fade out (`withAnimation` phases or `PhaseAnimator`); a new show replaces a running one. Reduce Motion (`NSWorkspace.shared.accessibilityDisplayShouldReduceMotion`, or Debug `-RoadieVisReduceMotion 1`): fade only, no scale. (#0242 handles the rest of Reduce Motion.)
- Probe `songTitleShows` counts shows.

### Readouts

- `settings.readouts: Set<VisReadout>`, toggled by F2–F5 (`toggleReadout`), persisted.
- `VisualizationReadoutsView`: top-left preset name (`vis.readout.preset`), top-right fps (`vis.readout.fps`, the display link's measured rate, smoothed, once a second), bottom-left "Artist – Title" (`vis.readout.title`), bottom-right "1:12 / 5:20" (`vis.readout.time`). Small (12 pt), shadowed. They stay when the overlay hides; while the overlay card is visible the two bottom ones hide (the card has the same) and the top-left one hides (the overlay's top strip has the preset name).
- Right-click menu: the four readout items and Show Song Title become enabled, with ✓ per readout.

### Help

- F1 or `?` (`help`) toggles `isHelpOpen`; `VisualizationHelpView` (`vis.help`): a centred card titled "Keyboard Shortcuts" listing `VisKeyMap.rows` (keys, then title) in two columns; Esc (already ordered first by #0237), F1, `?` or a click closes it. While open: overlay hold `.help`, auto-advance suspended. The right-click menu's Keyboard Shortcuts item opens it.
