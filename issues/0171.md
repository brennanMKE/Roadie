## Description

The website (title, tagline, hero, features, requirements) presents Roadie as "a Winamp-skinned player for Music Assistant" and lists a Music Assistant server as requirement 01. Roadie plays a local folder of songs on its own; Music Assistant is an optional extra for streaming its library and players. The copy should make the local music folder the primary, complete path, present Music Assistant as optional, and mention the first-launch flow (pick a music folder, get skins from the Winamp Skin Museum; being built in #0169).

## Expected behavior

- Title, meta descriptions, tagline and hero lead describe a Winamp-skinned music player for the Mac that plays your music folder.
- Features lead with the music folder; Music Assistant is one feature labelled optional.
- Requirements no longer list a Music Assistant server as required (optional item at the end).
- Top-level README.md matches. Prose only; layout and screenshot markup (#0170) untouched.

## Notes

- issues/Issues.md intro ("plays music from a Music Assistant server and local files") does not imply a requirement; left unchanged.
