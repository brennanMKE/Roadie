## Resolution notes

> 🟢 Resolved 2026-10-21 — Library context menus now say "Add to Queue" for the queue and offer an "Add to Playlist ▸" submenu (and drag-and-drop) that adds tracks and albums to Music Assistant playlists.

Review (Opus, phase 3): read `git diff b6d135c 756193c` against design §1.3, §2.3, §2.4 and W3, plus the two #0136 items in #0132's notes. "Add to Playlist" became "Add to Queue" in `libraryTrackActions` and `libraryAlbumMenu` (which `libraryAlbumActions` uses), so album tracks, playlist tracks, search, favorites, artist albums and all albums all say it. `AddToPlaylistMenu.entries` lists only playlists with `.addRemove`, the last three recent targets first, then the rest by name, leaves out the open playlist, and ends with "New Playlist…" (alone when there's no target). Recent targets are kept in UserDefaults (`library.playlists.recentTargets`) and recorded only on success. `add(_:toPlaylist:)` fetches the playlist, refuses one whose tracks can't be edited, and sends one `add_playlist_tracks` with the track and album URIs unchanged (artists dropped). The marquee gets "Added 1 album and 2 tracks to Road Trip" or the failure text. "New Playlist…" opens the name sheet, creates, then adds. `MediaIDTransfer` (in `MusicBackendKit`) uses the exported UTType `com.brennanmke.skinamp.media-id`, declared in project.yml/Info.plist after the skin type and checked by `check-info-plist.sh`. Albums and tracks are draggable; editable playlist rows and the open editable playlist accept drops. The Playlists list is loaded on connect when `.playlistEditing` is present, so the submenu works from any section.

#0132 notes: Discover didn't have Add to Playlist. The reviewer added it in 0da0673 (tracks and albums of the selection, per decision 3) and also made search results' tracks and albums draggable, which the design names as a drag source. Replacing #0140's `SaveQueueSheet` with `PlaylistNameSheet` is not trivial, so it wasn't done. Save Queue as Playlist… runs from the Playback menu while the Library window may be closed, and `PlaylistNameSheet` is a SwiftUI sheet hosted by that window's `PlaylistEditingModifier`. Leave it for #0146 (LIST › Save), which needs the same app-wide name prompt.

Minor, not blocking: File › "Add to Playlist…" (⌥⌘O, adds files to the local queue) still says playlist. The design's rename covered the Library context menus, and the File menu belongs to the PL window. There's no progress bar in the open playlist while adding; the marquee notice covers it until W4 adds the bar. Discover items aren't draggable.

## Root cause

The Library's only "Add to Playlist" appended to the queue, and nothing called `music/playlists/add_playlist_tracks`, so tracks couldn't reach a real playlist.

## Fix

- `MusicBackendKit`: `add(_:toPlaylist:)` (default `.unsupported`), `MediaIDTransfer` + `UTType.skinAmpMediaID`.
- `MusicAssistantKit`: `MARequest.addPlaylistTracks` and `MusicAssistantBackend.add` (read-only refusal, URIs passed through).
- `LibraryBrowser`: pure `AddToPlaylistMenu` (entries, recent ordering, notice text); `PlaylistsModel.add`, `create(named:adding:)`, recent targets; `loadPlaylistsIfNeeded`.
- App: `AddToPlaylistMenu` view, `PlayerModel.addToPlaylist/createPlaylist(named:adding:)`, `addsDroppedItems(to:model:)`; menus renamed and the submenu added in `LibraryPlaybackActions`; draggable album and track rows; drop targets on playlist rows and the open playlist; the UTType in project.yml/Info.plist and its check.
- Reviewer follow-up (0da0673): Add to Playlist in Discover's context menu; search results draggable.
